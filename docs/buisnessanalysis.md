# StayEase Business Analysis

## 1. Which room type is booked most often?

### Result

| Room Type | Booking Count |
|-----------|---------------|
| Standard  | 4             |
| Deluxe    | 4             |
| Suite     | 2             |

### Business Analysis

Standard and Deluxe rooms have the highest number of bookings,
with 4 bookings each. Suite rooms have 2 bookings. This shows
that Standard and Deluxe rooms have higher booking demand in
the given sample data.

## 3. What is the average length of stay?

### Result

Average Length of Stay: 2.6667 days

### Business Analysis

The average length of stay for non-cancelled reservations is
approximately 2.67 days. This means that guests in the given
sample data generally stay for around 2 to 3 days.
